# Travel Policy

## Overview

Northwind Robotics employees travel to customer sites, trade shows, and our test facilities. This policy sets out how to book, what to spend, and what to do when plans change. Travel should be necessary, safe, and cost conscious.

## Booking

All flights, hotels, and rental cars must be booked through the Voyager portal. Bookings made elsewhere are not covered by our corporate rates or by our travel insurance and will not be reimbursed except in an emergency.

Trips should be booked at least 14 days before departure. Fares usually rise sharply inside that window, so late bookings need a short justification in the portal that your manager approves.

Every trip needs a manager's approval in Voyager before any ticket is purchased. International trips additionally need approval from the department head.

## Flights

Economy class is required for flights under 6 hours, and premium economy is allowed on flights of 6 hours or longer. Business class requires written approval from a vice president and is normally granted only for overnight flights that end with a same-day customer meeting.

Choose the lowest logical fare within two hours of your preferred departure time. Frequent flyer miles belong to the traveler, but you may not pick a more expensive flight just to earn them.

## Hotels

The nightly hotel cap is 220 dollars in Tier 1 cities, 160 dollars in Tier 2 cities, and 120 dollars in Tier 3 cities. Tier 1 includes San Francisco, New York, and London, while most other large metropolitan areas are Tier 2. Voyager shows the tier for each destination when you search.

If the cap cannot be met because of a major conference, you may exceed it by up to 15 percent with manager approval recorded in the portal. Taxes and resort fees are included when comparing a rate with the cap.

## Meals on the Road

The daily meal allowance on trips is capped at 40 dollars each day, matching the expense policy. Meals provided by a conference or a customer are deducted from the allowance for that day.

## Ground Transportation

Rental cars must be compact or midsize and are booked with Harbor Rent-a-Car. Larger vehicles are allowed only when you are carrying demo equipment that will not fit in a smaller car. Decline the rental company's collision coverage, because the corporate policy already covers damage to rented vehicles.

Use trains, shuttles, or ride-share services for trips shorter than 30 miles when they cost less than a rental car. Refuel the car before returning it, because refueling charges from the rental company are not reimbursed.

## Travel Insurance

Trip Shield travel insurance covers up to 100,000 dollars in emergency medical costs and is included automatically for every trip booked through Voyager. It also covers trip cancellation costs for illness, with a limit of 3,500 dollars per traveler.

Save all medical receipts and send them to the Trip Shield claims line within 60 days of treatment.

## Changes and Cancellations

Cancel unneeded reservations in Voyager at least 24 hours before they start to avoid no-show fees. Unused airline tickets stay in your Voyager account as credits for twelve months, and the travel coordinator will remind you before they expire.

## Safety and Emergencies

Share your itinerary with your manager and save the emergency travel hotline, 555-0142, in your phone. The hotline is staffed around the clock by the Travel Desk and can rebook flights, arrange medical transport, and contact family members.

Employees should avoid traveling alone to remote test sites after dark, and anyone who feels unsafe may leave a site at any time without penalty.

## After the Trip

File your expense report promptly, using the process and deadlines in the expense policy. Include the Voyager trip number so Finance can match your bookings to the report automatically.
