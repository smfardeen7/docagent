# New Hire Onboarding

## Welcome

Welcome to Northwind Robotics. This guide describes what happens in your first weeks, who will help you, and what we expect from you as you settle in. Everyone on the team remembers being new, so please ask questions freely.

## First Week

New hires start on a Monday, and orientation begins at 9:30 AM with People Ops. You will meet your manager, receive your laptop, and take a tour of the office or a virtual tour if you work remotely. Lunch on the first day is provided by the team.

Tuesday is for team introductions and a walkthrough of the current roadmap. Wednesday is reserved for Security Basics training, followed by a hands-on tools workshop on Thursday. On Friday you have a one-hour check-in with your manager to discuss how the week went.

## Accounts and Tools

Your accounts are provisioned before your first day, including email, the Chatterbox messaging app, the Trackwell ticket system, and the PeopleHub HR portal. Engineers also receive access to GitHub Enterprise and the simulation cluster.

If something does not work on day one, reach out to the IT Helpdesk at extension 2200. Helpdesk staff can reset passwords and fix access problems in minutes.

## Onboarding Buddy

Every new hire is paired with an onboarding buddy for their first 60 days, and the two meet for 30 minutes each week. A buddy is a peer, not your manager. They answer practical questions, such as where to find the test lab and how to book a meeting room.

Buddies are volunteers who have been at Northwind for at least a year. If the pairing does not work well, tell People Ops and a new buddy will be assigned.

## Required Training

Three courses are mandatory for all new hires. Security Basics must be finished within the first 5 days. Code of Conduct training must be completed within 10 days of starting, and it covers harassment prevention, conflicts of interest, and reporting concerns.

Safe Robotics Handling is a practical course that must be done within 30 days of starting. It teaches how to power down a robot, use the emergency stop, and work safely near moving machines. Nobody may enter the test lab unsupervised until this course is complete.

## Probation

The probationary period lasts 90 days. During this time your manager gives you feedback at least every two weeks, and either side may end the employment relationship with one week of notice. A formal review takes place at the end of the period, and most employees pass without any issue.

## Your First 30, 60, and 90 Days

By day 30, you should have finished your setup, completed the required training, and shipped one small task. By day 60, you should own a modest project with clear goals that you agreed with your manager.

By day 90, you should present your work at the monthly all-hands demo session and propose one improvement to how your team operates. These goals are guidelines, and your manager will adjust them to suit your role.

## Who to Contact

For questions about pay, benefits, or leave, contact People Ops at people@northwind.example. For equipment or access problems, call the IT Helpdesk. For anything about your day-to-day work, ask your manager or your buddy first.

## A Few Tips

Keep a running list of questions during your first weeks and review it with your buddy. Join at least two interest channels in Chatterbox to meet people outside your own team. Take time to visit the product floor and watch an Atlas robot being assembled, because seeing what we build makes everything else easier to understand.
