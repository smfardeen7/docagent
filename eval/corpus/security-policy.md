# Security Policy

## Scope

This policy applies to every Northwind Robotics employee, contractor, and intern who uses company systems or handles company data. Our robots are connected to customer facilities, so a security lapse here can have physical consequences. The Security team owns this policy and reviews it every year.

## Passwords and Authentication

Passwords must be at least 14 characters long. We prefer passphrases made of several unrelated words, and we do not require periodic rotation unless a compromise is suspected. Store all credentials in the VaultKeeper password manager and never reuse a password across systems.

Admin accounts must use a hardware security key, while all other accounts use the Authenticator One app for multi-factor authentication. Text message codes are not permitted. If you lose your second factor, contact the IT Helpdesk to start an identity verification process.

## Device Protection

Every laptop is protected with CryptoVault full-disk encryption using AES-256. IT enables encryption before a device is handed over, and you must never disable it. Phones that access company email must also use a device passcode and remote wipe.

Screens lock automatically after 5 minutes of inactivity. Lock your screen manually whenever you step away, including at home and in the office.

Install operating system updates when prompted. Critical security patches must be installed within 72 hours of release, and devices that fall behind may be removed from the network.

## Networks

Use the TunnelOne VPN whenever you connect from a public Wi-Fi network such as a cafe, airport, or hotel. The VPN is not required inside company offices. Do not connect robots to guest networks, and do not plug unknown USB drives into any company device.

## Data Classification

Data is classified into four levels: Public, Internal, Confidential, and Restricted. Public data may be shared freely, Internal data stays within the company, and Confidential data is limited to people who need it for their work.

Restricted data includes customer site maps, robot firmware signing keys, and employee health records. It may be stored only in approved encrypted systems and must never be sent by personal email or messaging apps.

## Phishing

If you receive a suspicious message, do not click links or open attachments. Report suspected phishing with the Report Phish button in your mail client, or forward it to phish@northwind.example if the button is unavailable. After reporting, delete the message from your inbox.

The Security team triages every phishing report within 4 hours during business days. Employees who report real attacks are thanked in the monthly security newsletter, and we run simulated phishing campaigns each quarter to help everyone practice.

## Incident Response

Suspected security incidents must be reported to the Security Operations Center within 1 hour of discovery. An incident includes a lost device, a suspicious login, an exposed credential, or any unexpected robot behavior that may indicate tampering.

The Security Operations Center is staffed around the clock. For a Severity 1 incident, an on-call responder acknowledges the report within 15 minutes and opens a dedicated incident channel. Do not try to investigate on your own or delete evidence.

## Access Reviews

Managers review team access to Confidential and Restricted systems every quarter. Access is removed on the last day of employment, and contractors lose access automatically when their agreement end date passes.

## Consequences

Violations of this policy are handled by People Operations and may result in loss of access, mandatory retraining, or termination. Honest mistakes that are reported quickly are treated as learning opportunities rather than misconduct.
