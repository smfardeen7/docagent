# Remote Work Policy

## Philosophy

Northwind Robotics trusts its people to do great work from wherever they work best, as long as the work gets done and teams stay connected. This policy describes who can work remotely, when we expect people to be online, and what support the company provides.

## Eligibility

Remote and hybrid arrangements are open to roles in Software Engineering, Customer Support, Finance, and Marketing. Hardware technicians, lab staff, and manufacturing roles are not eligible because their work requires physical access to robots and test equipment.

New hires become eligible after completing their onboarding period, and arrangements are agreed in writing with the employee's manager. The arrangement is reviewed once a year.

## Working Hours

Core hours are 10:00 AM to 3:00 PM Eastern Time. During this window you should be available for meetings and quick questions, regardless of where you live. Outside core hours you may arrange your day to suit your life, provided you complete your agreed workload.

Update your calendar and your Chatterbox status when you step away, and mark any personal appointments as busy. Meetings that involve people in several time zones should be scheduled inside the core window whenever possible.

## Home-Office Stipend

Remote employees receive a one-time home-office stipend of 600 dollars, which can be renewed every 3 years. Use it for a desk, chair, lighting, or similar items that make your workspace comfortable. Submit receipts through the normal expense process.

Employees should not use the stipend for a laptop or a monitor, because those items are covered separately under the equipment policy.

## Internet and Phone

Northwind reimburses internet costs up to 50 dollars per month. Claim the amount quarterly and attach a copy of your bill showing the service address. Mobile phone costs are not reimbursed unless your role requires on-call availability, in which case your manager will arrange a stipend.

## Hybrid Schedule

Hybrid employees must be in the office 8 days per month. Teams usually choose shared anchor days so that in-person meetings, workshops, and lunches happen together. Check your team calendar for the agreed days and book a desk in the office app before you arrive.

Employees living more than 100 miles from an office may agree to a quarterly visit schedule instead, and the company covers the travel under the travel policy.

## Working from Another Country

Working from another country is allowed for up to 20 days per calendar year with HR approval. Request approval at least a month in advance, because tax, immigration, and insurance rules differ across countries. Approval is not guaranteed, and some countries are not permitted at all.

You remain responsible for being reachable during core hours while abroad, so choose a location with a reliable connection.

## Workspace Safety

Set up a workspace that is ergonomically sound, with a chair that supports your back and a screen at eye level. If you have a medical need for special furniture, contact People Operations and the company will discuss reasonable adjustments with you.

Do not hold confidential calls in public places, and use headphones when other people are nearby. Follow the security policy at all times when working from home.

## Equipment Return

Equipment must be returned within 14 days after the last day of employment. IT will email you a prepaid shipping label and a box for the laptop, charger, and any peripherals you were given. Items that are not returned may be deducted from the final paycheck where the law allows.

## Staying Connected

Remote teams hold a weekly video stand-up, and the whole company meets for a virtual all-hands on the first Thursday of every month. Managers should schedule regular one-to-one meetings with every remote team member and keep cameras optional.
