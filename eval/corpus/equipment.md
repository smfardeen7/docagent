# Equipment Policy

## Overview

Northwind Robotics provides the tools employees need to do their jobs. All devices remain company property, and each one carries an asset tag that IT uses to track location and warranty. This policy covers laptops, monitors, peripherals, repairs, and loss.

## Laptops

Engineers receive the Forge 16 laptop, and all other employees receive the Slate 14. The Forge 16 has a dedicated graphics processor for simulation work, while the Slate 14 is lighter and better suited to travel and office tasks.

Laptops are refreshed every 3 years for engineers and every 4 years for other staff. IT contacts you about three months before your device reaches the end of its cycle. Devices that fail early may be replaced sooner after a diagnostic by the IT team.

Old laptops are wiped by IT and recycled through a certified partner. Please do not attempt to sell or give away any company device.

## Monitors and Accessories

Office-based employees may claim a monitor allowance of 250 dollars. The allowance can be used again after 4 years, and the monitor you buy stays at your desk if you change teams. Remote employees should use their home-office stipend for furniture and are not eligible for the monitor allowance.

Keyboards, mice, and Echo Pro headsets are provided at no cost. Request them in the Trackwell catalog and they are normally delivered within three business days. Docking stations are available for hot-desking areas.

## Repairs

Report broken hardware by opening a Trackwell ticket in the Hardware category. FixPoint, our repair vendor, completes most repairs within 5 business days. A loaner laptop is issued within 1 business day of your ticket if the repair will take longer than that.

Back up your files to the company cloud drive before handing in a device. IT cannot guarantee the recovery of data stored only on a local disk.

Damage caused by negligence, such as spilling liquid, carries a 200 dollar deductible that is waived for the first incident. Normal wear and tear is always covered.

## Lost or Stolen Equipment

A lost or stolen device must be reported within 24 hours to the IT Helpdesk. IT will lock the device remotely and arrange a replacement once the report is filed. If theft is involved, a police report number should be added to the ticket.

Employees who repeatedly lose equipment may be asked to cover part of the replacement cost. Reporting promptly is always the right choice, and nobody is penalized for an honest first report.

## Acceptable Use

Incidental personal use is allowed, such as checking a personal calendar or streaming music while you work. Company devices may not be used for outside business ventures, mining cryptocurrency, or storing large amounts of personal files.

Only software from the approved catalog may be installed. Request anything else through IT so that licensing and security checks are completed first.

## Returning Equipment

Return all equipment when you leave the company or change to a role that uses a different laptop model. Office-based employees can drop devices at the IT desk, and remote employees follow the process in the remote work policy. IT checks every returned item against its asset tag and sends a confirmation email.

## Accessibility Needs

Employees who need specialized equipment, such as an ergonomic keyboard, a trackball, or a screen reader, can request it directly from the IT team without using their allowances. Requests are confidential and are usually fulfilled within ten business days.

## Contact

For equipment questions, open a Trackwell ticket or contact the IT Helpdesk. The IT team holds office hours at the second-floor desk each Tuesday and Thursday afternoon.
