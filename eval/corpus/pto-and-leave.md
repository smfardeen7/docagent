# PTO and Leave

## Purpose

Northwind Robotics wants employees to rest, care for family, and take part in their communities. This document describes paid time off (PTO), sick leave, parental leave, and other types of leave available to full-time employees. Part-time employees receive these benefits pro rata.

## Annual PTO

Employees in their first two years receive 15 days of PTO per year, employees in years three through five receive 20 days, and employees beyond five years receive 25 days. Your tenure is counted from your original start date, and the new allowance begins on your work anniversary.

PTO accrues at 1.25 days per month for new employees. Employees at the higher tiers accrue proportionally more, and your running balance is always visible in Timekeep.

## Carryover

Up to 5 days of unused PTO may be carried over into the next year, and carried-over days expire on March 31. Days above the cap are forfeited on December 31 and are not paid out. Employees who leave the company are paid for their remaining balance in their final paycheck.

## Requesting Time Off

Submit all requests in Timekeep. Requests of 3 or more consecutive days need 2 weeks of notice, and shorter absences should be requested as early as practical. Your manager will approve or decline within two business days.

During our annual product launch in the autumn, the Field Operations team may limit the number of people who can be away at once. Those blackout dates are posted in Timekeep by the end of June each year.

## Public Holidays

Northwind observes 11 company holidays each year. The full calendar is published in December for the following year. If a holiday falls on a weekend, the closest weekday is observed instead.

Employees who need to work on a holiday for customer support receive a replacement day to use within 60 days.

## Sick Leave

Each employee receives 10 paid sick days per calendar year. Sick days may be used for your own illness, medical appointments, or to care for a sick family member. Unused sick days do not carry over and are not paid out.

If you are sick for more than three consecutive days, a doctor's note is requested and should be sent to the People Operations team. Please notify your manager before your shift begins whenever possible.

## Parental Leave

Primary caregivers receive 16 weeks of paid parental leave, and secondary caregivers receive 8 weeks. The leave applies to birth, adoption, and foster placement. It may be taken in one block or split into two blocks within the first twelve months after the child arrives.

Employees should tell People Operations at least 60 days before the expected start date, or as soon as they know. Health benefits continue unchanged during leave, and employees return to the same role or an equivalent one.

## Bereavement Leave

Bereavement leave is 5 days for immediate family and 2 days for extended family. Immediate family includes a spouse, partner, child, parent, sibling, and grandparent. Additional unpaid time may be arranged with your manager if travel is required.

## Other Leave

Jury duty is paid for up to 10 working days, after which the employee may apply for unpaid leave. Northwind also provides 2 paid volunteer days per year for registered charitable work, and these do not count against your PTO balance.

Employees serving in a military reserve unit are granted leave for training and active duty as required by law. Contact People Operations to coordinate the paperwork.

## Questions

Questions about leave can be sent to the People Operations team at people@northwind.example. All medical information shared for leave purposes is kept confidential and visible only to the People Operations team.
