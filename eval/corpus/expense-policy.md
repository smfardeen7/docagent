# Expense Policy

## Purpose

Northwind Robotics reimburses reasonable business expenses that employees pay out of pocket while doing their jobs. This policy explains what is covered, how to claim it, and who must approve unusual costs. When in doubt, ask your manager before you spend, not after.

## Meals

Meals are reimbursed up to 40 dollars per day while you are traveling or working away from your home office. The cap covers breakfast, lunch, dinner, and snacks combined, so you cannot carry unused balance from one day to the next.

Client meals are capped at 90 dollars per person, and the most senior Northwind employee at the table should pay and file the claim. Alcohol is never reimbursable, even when it is part of a client dinner or a team celebration.

## Receipts and Documentation

A receipt is required for every single expense above 25 dollars. Photos of paper receipts are accepted as long as the date, vendor, and total are readable. For expenses below that amount, a short note describing the purpose is enough.

If you lose a receipt, fill in the Missing Receipt form and have your manager sign it. Finance allows no more than two missing receipt forms per employee per calendar quarter.

## Submitting a Claim

Expense reports must be submitted in Ledgerly within 30 days of the expense date. Reports filed after that window are returned, and the employee must request a late exception from the Finance Operations team.

Each line in the report needs a project code and a category. Managers review reports in the order they arrive and are expected to respond within three business days.

## Approvals

Any single expense above 500 dollars requires written approval from a director before you purchase it. Expenses above 5,000 dollars need sign-off from the Chief Financial Officer, and these requests should be sent to Finance Operations at least one week ahead.

Splitting a large purchase into several smaller ones to avoid an approval step is a violation of this policy and may lead to disciplinary action.

## Reimbursement Timeline

Approved reports are paid out within 7 business days of final approval. Payment is made by direct deposit to the bank account on file in PeopleHub. If a payment has not arrived after that period, contact Finance Operations with your report number.

## Mileage

Business use of a personal vehicle is reimbursed at 0.62 dollars per mile. Commuting between your home and your regular workplace does not count as business mileage. Record the starting address, the destination, and the odometer readings or a map screenshot in the claim.

Parking fees and tolls are reimbursed separately and need receipts when they exceed the receipt threshold described above.

## Corporate Card

The Northwind Card has a monthly limit of 5,000 dollars for most employees. It is intended for travel, software subscriptions, and approved team purchases, and it must never be used for personal items. Card transactions still require an expense report line with a receipt, and unreconciled charges are reviewed at the end of each month.

If your card is lost or stolen, freeze it in the Ledgerly app immediately and then notify Finance Operations.

## Non-Reimbursable Items

The following items are never reimbursed: fines for traffic or parking violations, personal grooming, entertainment for family members, gifts to government officials, and upgrades that were not pre-approved. Items that are merely inconvenient, such as a forgotten phone charger, may be reimbursed up to the meal cap discussed above only when approved by a manager.

## Questions

Send questions to finance-help@northwind.example. The Finance Operations team answers within one business day and keeps a running list of common questions on the internal wiki.
